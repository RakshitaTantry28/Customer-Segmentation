# 🧠 Data-Driven Customer Segmentation  
### Enhancing Marketing Strategies through Behavioral and Demographic Analysis  

## 📘 Overview  
This project leverages **machine learning and data analytics** to segment customers based on their **behavioral** and **demographic** patterns.  
The goal is to enable data-driven **marketing strategies**, identify **cross-selling opportunities**, and enhance **customer retention** through meaningful insights.  

---

## 🚀 Objectives  
- Collect and preprocess customer data for model readiness.  
- Engineer meaningful features representing customer behavior.  
- Identify customer groups using **K-Means Clustering**.  
- Apply **Apriori Association Rule Mining** to uncover frequent purchase relationships.  
- Derive actionable insights for targeted marketing and personalization.  

---

## 🧩 Tech Stack  
- **Language:** Python  
- **Libraries & Tools:** Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Mlxtend  
- **Algorithms:** K-Means Clustering, Apriori Algorithm  
- **Environment:** Google Colab  

---

## ⚙️ Workflow  

### 1️ Data Collection  
Gathered a dataset containing customer demographics and behavioral attributes such as purchase frequency, spending patterns, and product preferences.  

### 2️ Data Preprocessing  
- Handled missing values and outliers  
- Normalized numerical features  
- Encoded categorical variables  

### 3️ Feature Engineering  
Derived new attributes (e.g., total purchase frequency, recency, and average spending) to strengthen clustering effectiveness.  

### 4️ Clustering — K-Means  
Applied **K-Means Clustering** to discover natural customer segments with similar behavioral characteristics.  

### 5️ Association Mining — Apriori Algorithm  
Implemented **Apriori Association Rule Mining** to identify frequent itemsets and uncover potential cross-sell opportunities.  

### 6️ Visualization & Insights  
Visualized clusters using **Matplotlib** and **Seaborn** to interpret customer segments and their unique purchasing patterns.  

---

## 📊 Results & Insights  
- Successfully identified **distinct customer groups** based on behavioral and demographic features.  
- Extracted **association rules** for related products, providing a foundation for personalized marketing.  
- Enabled **data-backed decision making** for customer targeting and retention strategies.
